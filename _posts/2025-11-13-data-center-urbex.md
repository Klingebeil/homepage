<p class="mono-space small">[0:00] Camera turns on, showing a graffiti-tagged chain-link fence in the light of a flashlight. Dawn is visible on the horizon.</p>

<p><span class="mono-space small">TRANSCRIPT:</span> Alright, we're here. This is DataVault-7, one of the mega-facilities that powered the AI boom. Cost something like 8 billion dollars to build back in 2027. Been abandoned for… what, three years now? Since the crash.</p>

<p class="mono-space small">The camera pans across a massive concrete structure, windows dark, parking lot overgrown with weeds.</p>

<p><span class="mono-space small">TRANSCRIPT:</span> Let's see if we can get inside. Fucking hell, this is massive.</p>

<hr>

<p class="mono-space small">[2:15] Camera shakes as the operator climbs through a broken loading dock door. The interior is dim, emergency lights still faintly glowing red.</p>

<p><span class="mono-space small">TRANSCRIPT:</span> Jesus. Okay, we're in. You can still hear the hum. Some of the backup systems are still running on solar… I think?. Just... concrete and pallets. <span class="mono-space small">[kicks at debris]</span> Some old packaging materials. Cable spools.</p>

<p class="mono-space small">Camera pans across a mundane industrial space with peeling safety posters and a faded "DAYS WITHOUT INCIDENT: 847" sign.</p>

<p><span class="mono-space small">TRANSCRIPT:</span> Just from this space. You'd never know this was a billion-dollar facility. Could be any warehouse.</p>

<hr>

<p class="mono-space small"> [3:15] Walking through beige hallways with industrial carpet, some water-stained drop ceiling tiles above.</p>

<p><span class="mono-space small">TRANSCRIPT:</span> Trying to find a way into the actual server halls. These places had serious security… can't just walk in, usually. <span class="mono-space small">[tries a door]</span> Locked. <span class="mono-space small">[tries another]</span> Also locked.</p>

<p class="mono-space small">Passes break room with vending machines, motivational posters ("UPTIME IS EVERYTHING"), empty coffee station.</p>

<p><span class="mono-space small">TRANSCRIPT:</span> It's all so... corporate. Grey carpet, fluorescent lights, look at those sad office plants someone just left here.</p>

<hr>

<p class="mono-space small">[5:20] Descending concrete stairs, sound of steps echoing faintly.</p>

<p><span class="mono-space small">TRANSCRIPT:</span> Found a stairwell and some signage. This seems to be the right direction. <span class="mono-space small">[breathing heavily]</span> Going down.</p>

<p class="mono-space small">Camera shows emergency exit signs, still working. Camera focuses on graffiti: "THE GODS DIED HERE"</p>

<p><span class="mono-space small">TRANSCRIPT:</span> Someone's been philosophical about it. Can't say they're wrong, can you?</p>

<hr>

<p class="mono-space small">[6:30] Standing at a heavy security door, partially ajar.</p>

<p><span class="mono-space small">TRANSCRIPT:</span> Okay, here we go. Someone crowbarred this open already. Haha, look at the badge readers. <span class="mono-space small">[moves closer to the broken readers]</span> Standard data center security… yeah, that really worked out well.</p>

<p class="mono-space small">Squeezes through into a small vestibule, then through another door.</p>

<p><span class="mono-space small">TRANSCRIPT:</span> And... <span class="mono-space small">[pause]</span> ...yeah. Here's where the money went.</p>

<p class="mono-space small">Camera reveals rows upon rows of server racks stretching into darkness, some lonely LEDs blinking randomly like dying stars. The black towers recede into shadow, a mechanical cathedral built for silicon prophets who never came.</p>

<p><span class="mono-space small">TRANSCRIPT:</span> This place used to run 24/7. Hundreds of petaflops of computing power. They said it was training the models that would "change everything." <span class="mono-space small">[bitter laugh]</span> Yeah, well. Now, you're content.</p>

<hr>

<p class="mono-space small">[7:50] Walking through a canyon of black racks, stretching into the distance. Some panels open, copper wiring stripped.</p>

<p><span class="mono-space small">TRANSCRIPT:</span> This is what I wanted to see. <span class="mono-space small">[touches an emtpy server rack]</span> Scavengers got here first… or they sold some off when they shut down. GPUs are long gone, those things still sell for parts.</p>

<p class="mono-space small">Camera shows vinyl flooring, cable management trays overhead.</p>

<p><span class="mono-space small">TRANSCRIPT:</span> But most of this is just trash now. Obsolete before it even paid for itself. You can still hear the hum… backup power's running on autopilot, solar panels on the roof still feeding emergency systems, I think. Not sure if they have cameras down here.</p>

<p class="mono-space small">Picks up broken circuit board</p>

<p><span class="mono-space small">TRANSCRIPT:</span> Look at this… I know, it's broken but apart from that absolutely mint, just... discarded. The bubble burst so fast, they didn't even finish the decommission properly. Just locked the doors and walked away.</p>

<hr>

<p class="mono-space small">[12:40] Through another security door into a space with HVAC units, electrical panels, industrial shelving with spare parts.</p>

<p><span class="mono-space small">TRANSCRIPT:</span> Maintenance area. <span class="mono-space small">[camera shows logged inspection sheets on clipboards, last entry dated March 2032]</span> This is where the facilities guys worked. Could be in any office building.</p>

<p class="mono-space small">Points to wall of circuit breakers.</p>

<p><span class="mono-space small">TRANSCRIPT:</span> Except these breakers controlled megawatts. The power draw was insane.</p>

<hr>

<p class="mono-space small">[14:20] Descending more stairs into a massive concrete chamber with dormant cooling towers, industrial piping.</p>

<p><span class="mono-space small">TRANSCRIPT:</span> Okay, this is impressive. This is where they tried to keep everything from melting.</p>

<p class="mono-space small">Camera pans across industrial chillers, cooling towers, massive pipes labeled with flow directions. Steel arteries that once pumped rivers of coolant now stand still and cold, their purpose extinct.</p>

<p><span class="mono-space small">TRANSCRIPT:</span> These facilities used as much power as a small city. The environmental cost... <span class="mono-space small">[shakes head]</span> Just the cooling alone.
And when energy prices spiked in '32,the private asset market collapsed, and the promised "AI revolution" didn't materialize, investors just... pulled out. Overnight. Places like this went from billions in valuation to bankruptcy in months. The math never mathed, but the PowerPoints were gorgeous.</p>

<hr>

<p class="mono-space small">[18:10] Climbing stairs, breathing hard.</p>

<p><span class="mono-space small">TRANSCRIPT:</span> <span class="mono-space small">[panting]</span> These places are not designed for visitors. Everything's spread out... gotta be fit for this urbex thing.</p>

<p class="mono-space small">Passes emergency exit sign, continues climbing.</p>

<hr>

<p class="mono-space small">[21:15] In office area. Camera shows sunrise through regular aluminum-framed windows, light streaming across abandoned workstations with beige desk phones and wireless mice.</p>

<p><span class="mono-space small">TRANSCRIPT:</span> Control room. Someone left in a hurry. <span class="mono-space small">[picks up a laminated badge]</span> "Dr. Sarah Chen, Senior Engineer." Wonder where you are now, Sarah.</p>

<p class="mono-space small">Camera focuses on a whiteboard with equations and a note: "H100 cluster down AGAIN - cooling failure - projected loss: $2.3M/day"</p>

<p><span class="mono-space small">TRANSCRIPT:</span> They were burning through money so fast. Cooling costs alone were astronomical. And for what? Chatbots that couldn't turn a profit.</p>

<p class="mono-space small">Camera pans again over the office, looking out of the window onto an orange-streaked sky. Dawn breaks over the parking lot's cracked asphalt and wild grass, gilding the ruins with indifferent beauty.</p>

<p><span class="mono-space small">TRANSCRIPT:</span> So that's DataVault-7. A monument to irrational exuberance, haha. Somewhere in here, there are probably still SSDs with data for models nobody uses anymore. Dreams in silicon and copper, now just… rust and dust.</p>

<p class="mono-space small">Blows over the thick dust collected on a monitor.</p>

<p><span class="mono-space small">TRANSCRIPT:</span> You know what the mad bit is? There's hundreds of these places. All over. California, Texas, Ireland, Singapore… all dark now. Billions and billions spent, and for what? Makes you think about what we could've built instead, doesn't it?</p>

<p class="mono-space small">Starts walking toward exit.</p>

<p><span class="mono-space small">TRANSCRIPT:</span> Anyway, if you liked this one, smash that subscribe button. Next week I'm checking out an abandoned autonomous vehicle depot in Arizona. This is going to be a fun one, I think.</p>
