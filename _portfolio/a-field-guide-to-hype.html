Buzzword (noun)—Buzzwords are shorthands for complex technological fields, simplifying them with a catchy name (e.g. 5g, metaverse, artificial intelligence). They may develop from terms with specific meanings in specialist jargon and become fashionable among non-specialists, losing their precise meaning in the process. They’re often used to legitimize desired strategies, especially in settings removed from their original sectors of use.

The Gartner Hype Cycle

While Gartner’s hype cycle acknowledges the existence of hype, it still frames a technology’s success as inevitable. There is no option for failure or repeated hype cycles, as in the case of AI. Instead, it depicts innovation as a linear process without the messiness of the real world. Ironically, this makes the cycle part of the misleading rhetoric of hype.
