# Hype Literacy Toolkit

At the end of 2025 I was approached by the [*Hype Studies Group*](https://hypestudies.org/) to contribute to a “Hype Literace Toolkit”, an online course to teach media professionals how to deal with hype and hype cycles in the media industry. The project was funded by the *DW Akademie*.

The course was launched in April 2026 and is available for free [on the *DW Akademie* website](https://akademie.dw.de/hype-literacy/).

{% include img-full.html path="portfolio/hype-literacy-course/hype-literacy-course-teaser.png" alt="Cover Image" %}

## What was the challenge?

The group was approached with the question of how journalists could be better equipped to report on hyped topics, especially in the context of AI. The goal was to create a course that would help journalists understand the dynamics of hype and how to report on it in a more critical and informed way.

I was asked to contribute two hands-on sessions with concrete strategies in reporting on hyped topics, based on my research on technological hype and the media industry.

## Launch at the International Journalism Festival 2026

The course was officially launched at the [*International Journalism Festival*](https://www.journalismfestival.com/programme/2026/hype-literacy-reporting-on-ai-beyond-the-buzz) in Perugia, Italy, in April 2026.
