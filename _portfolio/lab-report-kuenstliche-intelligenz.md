# Lab Report: Künstliche Intelligenz

With the amount of attention AI, or “Generative AI” was receiving in 2023, we were tasked to create a report collection every “AI project” the *Media Lab Bayern* funded in the last years.

With 17 case studies on 29 pages and over 84,400 characters (13,800 words), the finished report is no quick read. I took about 4 weeks to write and design it, the work divided between Christian Simon (editorial), Bernd Oswald (editorial) and myself (editorial & design), in addition to the final proofreading, as well as a landing page and social campaign by the marketing team of the *Media Lab Bayern*.

## Writing the report

To speed up the process and structure the content we send out questionnaires every project team was able to fill out themselves. This approach simplified a lot of the design work and also made every individual case study predictable for the reader, as everyone was structured through the same questions. Still, a couple of hand picked projects received a more hands-on treatment in the form of longer and more detailed interviews.

To track the progress of the work we used a Google table, shared with every author and stakeholder, including information of the state of each case study, the corresponding authors, notes and contacts.

## Designing the report

The layout was based on a simple underlying grid. Images were also adapted to the grid and integrated using clipping masks.

The layout was thus structured and consistent, but allowed for variable and varied layouts.

Three additional colors to the brand colors of the *Media Lab Bayern* were used for the report in order to better distinguish chapters by color and at the same time keeping them seperate from existing branding elements. The colors were based on the colors of Post-Its. With the aim of presenting the work done in the Lab, the palette was complemented by a number of stock scribbles and arrows. A break with the core style guide of the lab, but a deliberate one that kept  the spirit of the *Media Lab*.
