# Adoption of Technology is weird

The classical “Diffusion of Innovation” model (Everett Rogers) suggests a fairly rational, S-curve–like pattern of adoption (innovators → early adopters → early majority, etc.)—but actual diffusion is generally very situated, contradictory, and weird.

Rogers (and to a similar extend the Gardener Hype Cycle) treat technologies as monolith—they have inherent properties (features, meanings, purposes) that determine adoption. Reality is a good bit messier. Instead of a clean process of adoption, social groups negotiate what a technology means and how it should be used.

They might find completely new uses, or completely reject a given tech.x
