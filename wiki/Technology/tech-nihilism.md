# Techno-Nihilism

**Techno-nihilism** views technological change as lacking inherent purpose or direction.  

Unlike *techno-optimism* (techno-financial[^1] regimes as a net positive) or *techno-pessimism* (techno-financial regimes as a source of harm), it emphasizes that innovation expands capabilities without guaranteeing meaning, improvement, or coherent progress. Techno-nihilism sees technology as a more neutral or chaotic force that expands capabilities without offering purpose, guidance, or answers about what those capabilities are for.

[^1]: I am referring here mostly to the contemporary discourse around technology, which seldom refers to an artifact, but instead a system of technologies and how their design and deployment is influenced by financial interests, a.k.a. startups and ‘big tech’.
