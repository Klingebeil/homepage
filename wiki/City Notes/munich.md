# Coffeeshops

- <span class="button-copiable" data-alias="Man vs. Machine Munich">Man vs. Machine</span>—The first _third wave coffee_ shop in Munich. Today in two locations. Best Franzbrötchen in town.
- <span class="button-copiable" data-alias="Sweet Spot Kaffee Munich">Sweet Spot Kaffee</span>—Also in two locations around Viktualienmarkt and Glockenbachviertel. Both are small so getting a seat in Fridays and the weekend can be tough. Big selection of imported specialty coffee.
- <span class="button-copiable" data-alias="Suuapinga Munich">Suuapinga Munich</span>—In multiple locations, ft. some very juicy cardamom buns.
- <span class="button-copiable" data-alias="Kanso Coffee Lab Munich">Kanso Coffee Lab Munich</span>—A bit hidden but the perfect location if you have every wanted to get really pretentious with coffee—in a good way. Splurge some money on the tasting options.
- <span class="button-copiable" data-alias="Loretta Munich">Loretta</span>—Solid coffee and enough tables, as wel as a stable wifi to get a bit of work in. Just make sure to tip your waiter.

# Bars

- <span class="button-copiable" data-alias="Bar Garçon Munich">Bar Garçon</span>—My personal favorite. It’s not the cheapest but perfect for a relaxed after work drink or start for the evening. Serving wine, snacks, and cocktails.
- <span class="button-copiable" data-alias="Loretta Munich">Loretta</span>—Big selection of cocktails, as well as snacks. Can get packed on weekends.

# Eat

- <span class="button-copiable" data-alias="Soul Kitchen Munich">Soul Kitchen</span>—Neapolitan Pizza with a younger crowd. Multiple vegetarian options.
- <span class="button-copiable" data-alias="Kulinariat Munich">Kulinariat</span>—Small but cozy little restaurant, only open on certain nights with a multi-course menu.
- <span class="button-copiable" data-alias="Usagi Munich">Usagi</span>Izakaya meets Boazn meets Punkrock. It’s not exactly fusion but small, tasty and also loud. Not for long chats over wine but good food over a quick beer or sake. Ramen on Fridays.
- <span class="button-copiable" data-alias="München72 Munich">München72</span>—Themed around the Olympic games of ’72 they will serve you a breakfast worthy of an athlete, as well as a solid Bavarian dinner menu.

# Shops

- <span class="button-copiable" data-alias="Soda Book Munich">Soda Books</span>—Great selection of indie magazines and artsy/design/architecture books. Also the only one in Munich, unfortunately.
