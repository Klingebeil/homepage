There‘s a *cliché* that is often used when talking about powerfull people in tech, such as the Elon Musk or Sam Altman. The cliché of the “masterful storyteller”. It is often used to justify the effect their visions have on the overall story Silicon Valley tells itself about the future, and thus on the broader economy.

But have you heard both speak? Like, actually speak? Neither is a “storyteller” nor a “masterful” one. Or to be precise, they can count as masterful storytellers if the term refers to their relation with the truth.

There‘s a much simpler explanation that doesn't rely on hagiographical great men treatments: these are wealthy, well-networked, powerful people. That‘s it. They are treated as believable because they speak from a position of influence and power and because they tell the stories investors in the valley like to hear.
